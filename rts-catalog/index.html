RTS and the games around it

A canon of real-time strategy and the formats that split off from it: squad tactics, real-time campaigns, Warcraft III and StarCraft custom games, mods, mobile, browser, and Roblox. A title is here because it defined a format, still has a large audience, or is the game people mean when they name that corner.

Not a player-count ranking

Audiences are not comparable across Steam, Battle.net, mobile stores, browsers, and Roblox. League of Legends, Honor of Kings, and the big mobile strategy games are larger than any base-building RTS. Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition is the classic RTS with a public Steam count, at about 17,000 average daily players in mid-2026. Age of Empires IV sits near 9,000 to 10,000 on Steam and also runs on Xbox and PC Game Pass. StarCraft II does not publish an equivalent number.

Where the formats came from

Herzog Zwei and then Dune II set a live match with a base and an economy. Command & Conquer and Warcraft industrialized it. StarCraft made asymmetry the sport. Age of Empires carried the same verbs into history. Total Annihilation carried them into scale, which is why Supreme Commander, Forged Alliance Forever, Zero-K, and Beyond All Reason still feel like one family.

The editors mattered as much as the matches. StarCraft Use Map Settings produced Aeon of Strife. Warcraft III's World Editor turned that into Defense of the Ancients, and from there into League of Legends and Dota 2, then Honor of Kings and Mobile Legends. The same lobby named tower defense, and Legion TD hopped from Warcraft III to StarCraft II's Squadron TD to Legion TD 2. Dota Auto Chess, a Dota 2 custom, became Teamfight Tactics and the auto-battler wave within a year.

Mobile split the fantasy into two products. Clash of Clans is a village you raid on your own time. Clash Royale is a two-minute real-time match. A parallel 4X-war market, Rise of Kingdoms and later survival games such as Whiteout Survival and Last War, grew larger than most PC RTS. On Roblox the mass-audience version is tower defense, led by Tower Defense Simulator, with a newer layer of country-conquest games such as Control Europe.
