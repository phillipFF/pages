Takeaways

  1. Retention is down, not a wash

    Concurrent calendar D1 from Sep 18–22 is 8.5% (10/118) vs Control 18.4% (83/450). Rolling 24–48h D1 is 8.0% vs Relay 17.8% and Barracks 13.1%. Calendar D3 is 8.6% (5/58) vs Control 12.4% and Relay 12.5%. Unlike the last two prototypes, coming back is the problem.

  2. More people start a fight. They play less of one.

    First-six-hour combat start is 85.6% vs 53.2% Control (p<.001). Attempts per starter are 3.69 vs 8.71. Combat minutes per assigned are 7.2 vs 12.3. Median D0 playtime is 6.6 vs 11.9 minutes. RPG front-loads entry, then the session is short.

  3. Match 1 always wins. A quarter never finish it.

    Chronological attempt 1: RPG win 100% vs 88.7%, completion 74.5% vs 97.2%, duration 93s vs 106s (all p<.001). Attempts 2–3 stay near-certain wins, then attempt 4 win rate meets Control (85.7% vs 86.3%) while fights run longer (186s vs 155s).

  4. The funnel inverts after Match 3

    RPG is ahead at Match 1 (86% vs 54%) and still ahead at Match 3 (43% vs 38%). By Match 5 it is behind (20.8% vs 28.1% Control, 29.0% Relay). Match 5 win looks like Relay (59.5% vs 57.9% vs Control 39.2%), but fewer people get there, and Match 6–7 wins fall off.

  5. Playing more matches does not buy D1

    On Control and Relay, D1 climbs with D0 depth: 16–20% after 1–3 matches, 28–40% after 4–7, ~50–57% after 8+. RPG stays flat: 5.9% after 1–3, 9.5% after 4–7, 33% after 8+ (3/9). Depth is not converting into return the way it does on the other arms.

  6. Tutorial telemetry is silent

    RPG fires zero tutorial_started / tutorial_completed events. Control is 99.6% start / 76.9% complete. Players still reach Match 1 at 86%, so FTUE is a different path or uninstrumented — not a 0% tutorial completion in the usual sense.
