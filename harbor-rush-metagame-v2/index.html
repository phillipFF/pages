Harbor Rush · Metagame v2 · 2

The metagame is simplified: the SLG part is removed, and what remains is three layers. The UGC world, where you sail between player-made islands. The ship, whose crew slots power up your Turf Master stats and which adventures on its own while you are offline. And live ops: daily and weekly bonuses, and competitions.

Harbor Rush · Metagame v2 · Room names illustrative · 9

The second layer is the ship. It is your home base, shown as a cutaway with rooms and crew, and it borrows from three games. From Wasteland RV Survival (我的末日房车): a moving home that you upgrade room by room. From the Arknights dormitory: crew who live in rooms you decorate and rest there. From Fallout Shelter: the side-on cutaway, where you assign people to rooms. The room names here are illustrative. The ship does two jobs, covered on the next two slides: its crew slots power up Turf Master, and it adventures on its own while you are offline.

Harbor Rush · Metagame v2 · 11

Offline, the ship adventures around on its own. It fights monsters, which levels the ship up, and brings back resources that you use to buff up the ship directly over time. A higher-level ship gets better offline resources.
