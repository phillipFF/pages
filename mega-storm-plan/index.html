Example A · Boarding

Turf Master, reimagined as ship vs. ship

What if today’s Turf Master became ship-to-ship combat, with units boarding the enemy ship and taking it over?

Rogue Waters

Cannons while closing in, then grid tactics across both decks.

Abandon Ship

Ram, throw boarding hooks, and fight the enemy crew up close.

Void War

FTL-style ships; boarding sleds punch through the enemy hull.

Screenshots: Steam store listings

Example B · Artillery

Ships lobbing shots at each other

Another option: trade arcing shots between two ships, like Castle Busters, Forts or GunBound.

Castle Busters

1v1 PvP: load your keep and blast the rival castle to rubble.

Forts

Build a fort, then trade physics-based artillery fire.

GunBound

Turn-based shots: set angle and power, read the wind.

Screenshots: App Store (Castle Busters), Steam (Forts, GunboundM)
