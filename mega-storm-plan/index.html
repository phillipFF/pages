Example A · Boarding

Guidelines

Guidelines for every ship combat concept

01

Short

Async and/or skippable in under 30 seconds

02

On theme

Fits Mega Storm’s theming around ships and ship combat

Harbor Rush: Mega Storm

03

Derived

Ideally from Turf Master (not a must)

Or: Wasteland RV + a combat layer

04

Replayable

A good balance between RNG and strategy

  • Can’t feel too random
  • Rewards some skill or thinking
  • Factors in your Turf Master account power

Idea 1 of 11

Broadside Duel

From Castle Busters · Account power: cannon damage, hull HP, ship parts

Castle Busters

30 seconds · Async

Quick lob

How you play

  1. Drag back from your cannon to set angle and power
  2. Trade 3 shots with a ghost of a real player’s ship
  3. Most hull damage wins; skip to auto-resolve

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: read the wind and pick ammo

RNG: wind changes every turn, and the swell bobs both ships

3 minutes · Live PvP

Siege duel

How you play

  1. Both ships are cutaways, with crew in the helm, cannon deck, magazine and quarters
  2. Tap a room to target, then aim; 10 seconds a turn
  3. Knock out a room’s crew: no gunners, no return fire; the magazine crits

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: which rooms to hit first, and when to repair

RNG: wind and swell

Idea 2 of 11

Harbor Raid

From Clash of Clans · Account power: crew levels vs. harbor tower levels

Clash of Clans

30 seconds · Async

Snapshot raid

How you play

  1. Scout a snapshot of a real player’s Turf Master harbor
  2. Drag crew cards onto landing zones
  3. Crew auto-fight toward the loot; 30-second timer

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: where and when to land, and which crew to send

RNG: which crew the towers target first

3 minutes · Live PvP

Raid race

How you play

  1. You and a live opponent raid each other’s harbors at once
  2. Split crew between attacking and recalling to defend
  3. Most loot when the 3-minute timer ends wins

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: when to push and when to defend, read from their landings

RNG: which crew the towers target first

Idea 3 of 11

Deck Auto-Battler

From Super Auto Pets and Mechabellum · Account power: crew unit and ship spell levels

30 seconds: Super Auto Pets

Live: Mechabellum

30 seconds · Async

One shop, one fight

How you play

  1. Buy crew from a 3-card shop with gold
  2. Drag them onto deck slots; merge duplicates to level up
  3. Tap Fight: a 10-second auto boarding fight vs. a ghost

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: placement, crew synergies, when to reroll

RNG: what the shop offers, and which ghost you face

3 minutes · Live PvP

Wave war

Feels like Mechabellum

How you play

  1. Waves alternate against a live opponent: you place, then they do
  2. Merge matching crew to level up and wipe out their squads
  3. Cast ship spells (barrage, smoke, grapple); win 3 of 5 waves

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: countering their last wave, when to merge, spell timing

RNG: which crew you’re offered each wave

Battleship Volley

From Battleship · Account power: volley size and sonar charges

Battleship (Hasbro)

30 seconds · Async

Volley rush

How you play

  1. Tap 3 cells on a hidden ghost fleet, then fire the volley
  2. Hits and misses mark the grid; sonar reveals a 2×2 area
  3. Sink the fleet in 4 volleys for a full score

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: deduction and where to spend sonar

RNG: first shots, and where the fleet is hidden

3 minutes · Live PvP

Fleet battle

Classic Battleship, live

How you play

  1. Place your fleet, then trade shots with a live player
  2. 10-second turns; specials like barrage and decoys
  3. First fleet sunk loses

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: fleet placement, deduction and decoy bluffs

RNG: first shots before you have any hits

Idea 7 of 11

Cannon Dice

From Dicey Dungeons · Account power: number of dice, slot upgrades

Dicey Dungeons

30 seconds · Async

Roll and slot

How you play

  1. Roll 3 dice at the start of each turn
  2. Drag dice into slots: guns need 4+, sails dodge, a pair boards
  3. Read the enemy’s intent; sink it within 3 turns

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: where to spend each die against the enemy’s intent

RNG: the dice rolls

3 minutes · Live PvP

Dice duel

Yahtzee holds, simultaneous reveal

How you play

  1. Both players roll 5 dice at the same time
  2. Hold and reroll twice, then lock dice into slots
  3. Reveal together: guns hit, sails dodge, boarding steals a die; 5 rounds

RNG vs. strategy

Strategy: hold or reroll odds, and guessing their lock-in

RNG: the dice rolls
