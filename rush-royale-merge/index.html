Why this game

Growth you can drag

Rush Royale is a PvP tower defense from IT Territory and My.Games. Troops stand on a small grid and hold a lane of monsters. Kills pay mana, and mana is the currency of the match.

That mana grows the board in two ways.

  • Merging spends two troops to raise one rank.
  • Upgrading spends mana to raise every troop of a single type.

A deck of five is the list both actions draw from.

An RTS tech tree asks a player to spend for a better line of production. This game makes that spend a placement. The drag, the ladder, and the choice of which line to feed are the parts worth carrying into our title.

Rank always goes up by one. Which card appears is drawn again from the whole deck, including the pair you just spent. Two archers can become a rank-2 of any of the five.

Reasons for merging

  • Searching for a particular unit to counter opponents.
  • A specific ability or passive needed for the player's strategy.
  • Running out of space on your own playable tiles.

02 · Merge troops

Climbing compresses the board

4Base output of the two rank-2 troops you spend. Each one is worth two rank-1s.
→
3Base output of the rank-3 that replaces them. One body, one tile freed.

Guides agree a merge drops total damage until the empty tile is filled. The usual model is base output × rank, then an upgrade bonus on each troop. Confirm the multiplier on a current build before treating it as a tuning number.

Why a player still merges

  • Free a tile so the next summon has somewhere to land.
  • Draw a different card when the troop on the board is the wrong one.
  • Hit a rank threshold on a support whose effect cares about the rung more than the body count.
For our ladder. A tier that only returns the strength it consumed is a rung players leave alone. The step up has to change what that unit or building does.

Ways to aim the gesture

Goal: Explore Bigger Units or Better Buildings

Units · a body ladder

Two become a larger one

Tier 1

Soldier. The copy you deploy.

Tier 2

Veteran. Two soldiers fuse. Can now attack air units. Tankier.

Tier 3

Champion. Two veterans fuse. Access to new passive skill.

Buildings · a structure ladder

Two become the next tier

Tier 1

Barracks. Produces the base infantry.

Tier 2

Barracks II. Two barracks fuse. Spawns now produce Veteran units.

Tier 3

Barracks III. Two of the second tier. Spawns now produce Champion units.

Potential reasons to merge in our playable

  1. Output of better units in the same time frame. Better stats, better skills.
  2. Output of different units to quickly pivot production type for countering (or being countered).
  3. Healing or saving units from killing blows with good merge timings.

03 · Upgrade all troops of a type

Upgrading: Enhancing your most popular units

The number under a card spends mana to upgrade every troop of that type, the ones on the board and the ones still to be summoned. L.1 on the portrait is the card's level. The shape around a troop on the board is the merge rank.

Together they are a tree. Merge climbs one token and can change the card. This spend keeps the card and thickens every copy. Mana poured into one card makes that card the plan.
